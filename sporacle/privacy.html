Sporacle privacy policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Sporacle is a free, non-commercial app that identifies mushrooms from photos for learning purposes. It is made by an individual developer in Ireland. This policy explains what the app does with your data.

No account, no tracking

Sporacle has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not sell or share personal data.

Photos you identify

When you identify a mushroom while online, the photos you chose (up to three) are sent over an encrypted connection to the Sporacle identification server, which runs the identification model and returns the result. The server does not keep your photos after answering. It applies a rate limit by IP address, and the hosting provider keeps standard short-term server logs (IP address, time, request size).

When you identify on the phone (automatically when there is no connection, or always if you choose it in Settings), nothing is sent anywhere.

Feedback you choose to send

If you tap "Was this right?" and send feedback, we store your verdict, the species the app showed you, and any comment you typed. Your photos are included only if you tick the box to send them. Feedback is stored on the Sporacle server so we can improve the species safety notes and the model's ranking. It is not linked to an account, name or precise location.

Your journal and location

Your journal (identifications, photos, notes and habitat) is stored only on your phone. Recording where you found each mushroom is off by default. If you turn it on, the location is saved with the journal entry on your phone and shown on the map of finds. Location is never sent to the Sporacle server.

Third-party services the app contacts

  • iNaturalist (api.inaturalist.org): species pages load Creative Commons photos and links from iNaturalist. iNaturalist receives the request, including your IP address, under its own privacy policy.
  • OpenStreetMap (tile.openstreetmap.org): the map of finds loads map tiles from OpenStreetMap, which receives the tile requests, including your IP address, under its own privacy policy.
  • Shorebird: the app checks for over-the-air updates to its own code. Shorebird receives the app version and standard request metadata (such as IP address).
  • Sharing: "Ask an expert" uses your phone's share sheet. What you share, and with whom, is up to you and the app you share to.

Permissions

  • Camera and photos: to take or choose the photos you want identified. Photos are accessed only when you pick them.
  • Location (optional): only if you turn on "Save location" in Settings, to record where you found a mushroom.
  • Internet: to contact the identification server and the services listed above.

Children

Sporacle is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Deleting your data

Delete journal entries in the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything stored on your phone. To ask for feedback you sent to be deleted, contact us at the address below.
